Privacy policy

The Sheldon app (version 2.7.0). Effective from 22 October 2026.

Who is responsible for the data

The data controllers are the authors of the app: Jakub Wójcik and Szymon Górski. For privacy matters, write to JQWojcik@gmail.com or use “Settings → Write to the authors”.

What the app stores on your phone

The app needs no account or login. It stores on your phone:

  • liked words, collections, notes and hidden words,
  • browsing history and stats (how many words you viewed and liked on a given day, your word-of-the-day streak),
  • your interest in categories, which the app uses to order words (how it works is explained in the “Terms of use”),
  • flashcard progress, quiz results and earned badges,
  • sentences you write in the “Use the word in a sentence” exercise,
  • settings, such as the theme, text size, chosen topics and notification time,
  • the app’s error log (if it is turned on).

This data is not sent to the authors or to any server. You can delete it in “Settings → Your data → Delete all data” or by uninstalling the app.

Downloading new words

In this version all words are built into the app — it does not download them from the internet. Links to dictionary entries open in your browser only when you tap them.

Ads

The version with ads uses Google AdMob. Google may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads shown. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom see Google’s consent form before any ads. You can change your choice in “Settings → Privacy → Ad consent”. Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The ad-free version (an in-app purchase) does not load ads.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. The app never sees your card or payment account details. It stores the information that you bought the ad-free version on your phone.

Notifications, reading aloud and the widget

Word of the day notifications are scheduled by the phone itself. Reading aloud uses the phone’s built-in speech synthesiser. The widget shows the word of the day stored on your phone. None of these features sends data to the authors.

Backups

Android may save the app’s data in your Google account backup, and iPhone in its iCloud backup, according to your phone’s settings. You create the favourites backup file yourself and decide where to save it or whom to send it to.

Messages to the authors

When you send a mistake report, a word suggestion, an error report or a message, the authors receive its content and the sender details visible in the app you choose (e.g. your email address). We use them only to reply and to improve the app.

Legal bases and retention

  • Data stored on your phone is not sent to the authors. It stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Messages to the authors are processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): to reply and improve the app. We keep them until the matter is resolved, for no longer than 2 years.
  • Personalised ads in the European Economic Area and the UK are shown only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Google processes this data as a separate controller under its own terms.
  • In-app purchases are handled by the store (Google Play or the App Store) as a separate controller.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland. You can delete the data stored on your phone yourself in the app’s settings.

Changes

We will announce changes to this policy in the app’s update notes.
